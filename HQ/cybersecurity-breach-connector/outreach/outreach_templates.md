# Cybersecurity Breach Connector: Outreach Templates

When executing the connector model, the tone is everything. You are reaching out to executives who are currently in crisis mode. 

**DO NOT:** 
- Be overly salesy.
- Induce panic or act like you're hacking them.
- Send lengthy marketing emails.

**DO:**
- Be highly empathetic and professional.
- Mention you saw the public filing (removes the "how do you know" fear).
- Offer a highly vetted, immediate solution (an IR team ready to deploy).
- State clearly that you are a broker/connector who has done the vetting for them.

---

## Template 1: The Direct "Speed to Remediation" Approach
**Best for:** CEOs, CTOs
**Subject:** The recent incident at {{company_name}} / immediate IR availability

Hi {{first_name}},

I saw the recent data breach notification filed with the State Attorney General regarding {{company_name}}. I know exactly how chaotic the first 48 hours of containment can be.

I'm reaching out because I maintain a network of elite, vetted Incident Response (IR) teams that specialize in rapid deployment. Many firms right now have a 1-2 week waitlist for intake, but I have a specialized team available immediately that handles exactly the type of incident you are managing.

If you are currently struggling with your existing IR vendor's response times, or if you haven't retained one yet, I can facilitate a warm introduction right now to get boots on the virtual ground today. 

Let me know if you need this resource. 

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]

## Template 2: The "Second Opinion / Augmentation" Approach
**Best for:** CISOs, VP of IT
**Subject:** State AG notification / IR augmentation for {{company_name}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I'm reaching out regarding the recent data security incident notification for {{company_name}}. 

Assuming you already have an IR firm engaged, you may still be facing resource bottlenecks on the containment and forensics front, especially if it's a widespread ransomware or complex exfiltration case. 

My role is to connect organizations in active crisis with highly specialized, vetted cybersecurity talent and fractional IR teams. If your current team is overwhelmed or if you need a second opinion on the forensic containment strategy, I can make an immediate introduction to a Tier-1 IR unit that is on standby.

Happy to make the connection if you need the extra firepower right now.

Stay resilient,

[Your Title]
